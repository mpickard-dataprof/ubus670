Riverside Accounting LLP Employee Expense Policy effective January 2026 replaces the prior policy dated March 2023 all employees are expected to review this document and acknowledge receipt via the HR portal within 14 days of issue. Meals during business travel are reimbursed at actual cost up to a daily cap of $75 per person for domestic travel and $125 per person for international travel receipts are required for any single meal over $25 alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances including client meals. Client meals where a client is physically present and where business is discussed are reimbursable at actual cost up to $100 per person per meal with a receipt and a written note identifying the client the business purpose and the names of all attendees alcohol consumed at a client meal is reimbursable up to 25 percent of the meal total but never exceeding $50 per meal. Team meals meaning meals attended exclusively by Riverside Accounting employees for team-building retention or celebration purposes are reimbursable only with prior written approval from a partner and only up to $60 per person. Ground transportation including rideshare taxi and personal car mileage at the IRS standard rate is reimbursable when used for business purposes rental cars are reimbursable at the economy rate unless a larger vehicle is required for equipment or multiple passengers. Lodging is reimbursable at actual cost with a nightly cap of $250 per night domestic and $400 per night international. Home office expenses are not reimbursable under this policy see the separate remote work policy. Airfare is reimbursable at economy class for flights under six hours and business class for flights over six hours. Any expense not explicitly listed in this policy requires pre-approval from a partner submit the expense plan to your supervising partner before incurring the expense. All receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date via the Concur expense portal. Questions should be directed to finance at riverside dot example.
