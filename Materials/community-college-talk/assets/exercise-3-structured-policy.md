# Riverside Accounting LLP — Employee Expense Policy

**Effective:** January 2026
**Replaces:** Prior policy dated March 2023
**Acknowledgment:** All employees must acknowledge receipt via the HR portal within 14 days.

---

## Meals

### Travel Meals (while on business travel)
- **Domestic cap:** $75 per person per day
- **International cap:** $125 per person per day
- **Receipts:** Required for any single meal over $25
- **Alcohol:** Not reimbursable under any circumstances

### Client Meals (a client is present AND business is discussed)
- **Cap:** $100 per person per meal
- **Required documentation:** receipt, business purpose, list of all attendees, client name
- **Alcohol:** Reimbursable up to 25% of meal total, never exceeding $50 per meal

### Team Meals (Riverside employees only)
- **Cap:** $60 per person
- **Approval required:** Written approval from a partner before the meal

---

## Transportation

| Type | Reimbursable? | Limit |
|------|---------------|-------|
| Rideshare / taxi | Yes | Actual cost for business use |
| Personal car mileage | Yes | IRS standard rate |
| Rental car | Yes | Economy rate (larger vehicle only if justified) |
| Airfare (under 6 hrs) | Yes | Economy class |
| Airfare (over 6 hrs) | Yes | Business class |

---

## Lodging

- **Domestic cap:** $250 per night
- **International cap:** $400 per night

---

## Not Covered by This Policy

- **Home office expenses** → see separate Remote Work Policy
- **Any expense not explicitly listed** → requires pre-approval from your supervising partner before incurring

---

## Submission

- **Tool:** Concur expense portal
- **Deadline:** All receipts within 30 days of expense date
